FAQ

Before you apply

Still unsure? Write to @rustamovabduaziz on Telegram.

Is my application sent automatically?

No. The site prepares your application and registration number, then you send it yourself in Telegram. This keeps all communication in one direct chat with the instructor.

What does the course cover?

The full SAT Reading & Writing section across all four domains: Information & Ideas, Craft & Structure, Expression of Ideas, and Standard English Conventions. It does not cover SAT Math.

When are classes?

Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday. The instructor shares exact times with the group. Homework is due on class days at 19:59 Uzbekistan time.

I've paid. Where are the lessons?

In the Student area on this site. After payment the instructor sends you a personal access code. Open the Student area, enter the code and your first name, and everything unlocks.

How and when do I pay?

Only after the instructor confirms your place. Payment is arranged one-on-one in your Telegram chat with @rustamovabduaziz.

Do I need an SAT score to apply?

No. Students who haven't taken the SAT yet are welcome. A practice test score helps the instructor place you, but it's optional.
